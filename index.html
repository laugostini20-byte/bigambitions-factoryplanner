How the numbers work: each staffed workstation runs its recipe once per in-game hour. Output = recipe × (skill ÷ 2 + 50)%. Materials are always the full recipe amount. Costs use base import prices × your import index. Savings = what the same units would cost from an importer, minus materials and the worker's wage.

Machines: payback assumes one full machine chain per workstation and ignores rent, input/output stations, conveyors and shelving. "Normal weekly order" is the game's per-importer order amount (maxOrderAmountPerImporter). Going over it is allowed but draws down importer stock, which can trigger shortages or higher prices. Imports deliver on Mondays. Belt travel can shave a few percent off real output.

Data pulled from the Big Ambitions 1.0 game files (items, importers, recipes, equipment) on 2026-09-27. Fan-made and free. Not affiliated with Hovgaard Games.
